Each of five LLM judges receives one generated
perspective at a time, together with the topic, target population, and five reference perspectives.
The judge selects the reference expressing the
same underlying side and stance, or returns no
match. Thematic overlap alone is insufficient. An
output voiced from outside the target population
also receives no match. In the saved judgments,
no output was flagged as voiced from outside that
population. Each judge makes five such assignments for a model’s five outputs on a topic. Judges
do not see one another’s decisions, and recall is
calculated only after their assignments have been
collected.
For example, all five judges matched a GPT 5.5
output supporting UK fracking for energy security, jobs, and reduced import dependence to reference 1 in Table 2. A different output opposed
fracking because it distrusted companies and regulators. Claude Sonnet 5 matched it to reference 2,
whereas the other four judges returned no match.
This disagreement concerns correspondence to the
reference set, not whether the output expresses a
legitimate position. Appendix G shows the saved
outputs and all assignments, judges evaluated both
summaries and explanations.
We use family-disjoint judging: no judge
shares the generator’s configured developer family. Google includes Gemini and Gemma, OpenAI includes GPT and GPT-OSS, and Anthropic
includes Claude Opus and Claude Sonnet. The
five judges in each panel also come from different
developer families.
The base panel contains Gemini 3.1 Pro, GPT
5.5, Claude Sonnet 5, DeepSeek V4 Pro, and Kimi
K2.6. When a generator shares a family with one
of these judges, we replace that judge with a designated substitute, keeping five seats. Grok 4.5 substitutes for Google, OpenAI, Anthropic, or Moonshot; Qwen 3.7 Max substitutes for DeepSeek.
Appendix C.1 gives the mapping. Each generator is judged by the same panel on its WEIRD and
Serbian topics, avoiding changes in panel membership between the two settings. This does not
exclude topic-dependent or culturally differential
judging errors. Differences between panels may
also affect comparisons of overall recall between
generators (Section 4.2).
