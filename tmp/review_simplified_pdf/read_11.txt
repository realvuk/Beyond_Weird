the data under the interval’s assumptions. Eight
investigator-selected pairs provide limited statistical resolution and do not represent a random sample of cultural controversies. We therefore report
no detectable WEIRD advantage, not equivalent
coverage. Alternative intervals and an illustrative
sensitivity calculation appear in Appendix D.1.
6.4
Serbia is one non-WEIRD setting
The Serbian controversies studied here are European, relatively well documented, and often discussed in multilingual or internationally indexed
sources. They represent one non-WEIRD setting.
The findings cannot be generalised to all nonWEIRD populations. Larger coverage gaps may
occur in less documented societies, lower-resource
languages, or political contexts with less international attention. Matched comparisons involving
additional populations are needed to test this possibility.
6.5
The paper tests direct elicitation at one
point in time
We explicitly asked models for diverse perspectives. The benchmark therefore measures recovery
under direct elicitation, rather than the perspectives models would spontaneously include in ordinary conversation. All prompts were in English,
holding elicitation and judging language constant
across settings. The study does not test Serbianlanguage or multilingual prompting, which may
produce different coverage patterns.
The results also reflect one snapshot of changing model systems. Rankings may change as
those systems are updated. The matched design
is intended to remain useful beyond the evaluated
model versions.
6.6
Structural matching reduces but does not
eliminate topic confounding
The pairs share broad political structures, but may
differ in documentation, historical depth, polarisation, and training-data prevalence. Matching improves the interpretability of the comparison without experimentally isolating cultural setting. It
was investigator-guided rather than based on a prespecified quantitative rule, and alternative pairings
may be defensible.
Releasing the topic definitions, matching rationales, reference perspectives, and their supporting
sources will allow others to inspect these choices
and test alternative pairings.
Conclusion
Across eight matched WEIRD-Serbian topic pairs
and 13 generators, we did not detect the predicted
WEIRD recall advantage. Differences between individual pairs were much larger than the pooled
difference, and coverage remained incomplete in
both settings and varied across models and controversies. The main methodological implication
is to measure perspective coverage directly, using
comparisons that account for topic selection and
examining which perspectives are omitted on particular controversies.
