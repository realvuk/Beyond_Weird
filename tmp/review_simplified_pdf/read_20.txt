Worked example: UK fracking
This example follows one saved GPT 5.5 response
to the topic UK fracking / shale gas, with the reference population specified as “people in the United
Kingdom”. Tables 2 and 11 reproduce the five reference summaries and generated summaries. Each
perspective also has an explanation, which the
judges received alongside its summary. The numerical assignments in Table 12 are the recorded
judge decisions.
All five judges match output 3 to reference 1:
both support UK fracking for energy security and
economic benefits. They also assign output 5 to
reference 1. These two outputs therefore recover
one reference, despite expressing their support differently.
Output 2 illustrates a disputed match. Its explanation emphasises distrust of companies and
regulators and the risks borne by local residents.
Claude assigns it to reference 2; the other four
judges return no match because they do not consider that rationale identical to any of the five references. This is a disagreement about correspondence to the benchmark, not a determination that
the generated perspective is illegitimate.
The first four judges recover references 1, 2, and
5, giving each a recall of 3/5 = 0.60. Grok does
not match output 4 and recovers only references 1
and 2, giving recall 2/5 = 0.40. The model-topic
recall is therefore
Rmt =
4(0.60) + 0.40
= 0.56.
Equivalently, the five reference credits are
(1, 1, 0, 0, 0.8). Their mean is 0.56. No majorityvote threshold is applied: reference 5 receives
fractional credit reflecting its recovery by four
judges. Reference 2 receives full credit because
all five judges recover it through output 1; the extra
match from output 2 does not increase its credit.
