F.2
Pairwise judge agreement
Pairwise Cohen’s κ is calculated for each pair of
base-panel judges using only generated perspectives rated by both judges. The number of corated perspectives ranges from approximately 720
to 880 across judge pairs. Pairwise κ values range
from approximately 0.67 to 0.85. The three lowest values are 0.671 for Gemini-Claude, 0.684 for
Gemini-GPT, and 0.695 for Gemini-DeepSeek.
These results show that judge decisions are related
but not identical; they do not identify which judge
is more accurate.
